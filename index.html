11 / Start small enough to learn12 / 14

One product.
A capped experiment.

PROPOSED SCOPE≤100 units

One tee. One or two colours.
Deliberate size curve.
New existing stock bought wholesale.

No lease · no private-label run
  1. Before a buyApprove evidence, sample fit, actual landed quote and total cash/loss cap.
  2. During a six-week selling testCompare presentation; record full-price sell-through, basket and costs.
  3. After the returns windowReconcile contribution, refunds and unsold stock. Reorder, revise or stop.

100 is a ceiling to consider—not an order, MOQ or validated budget. Smaller batches may cost more than the 500-unit economics example.

Proposal only. Agree a numeric cash/loss cap before spending; no commitment authorised. Decision gates ↗
