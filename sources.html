Slides 02–04

A local-access hypothesis, not an empty market.

Selected existing labels might gain useful access to Icelandic customers through a locally relevant curated webshop. This is a strategic hypothesis, not verified demand or a claim that any named label is absent from Iceland. Check local stockists, cross-border consumer access, comparable product quality, full delivered prices and service before asserting an opportunity. No population-to-sales forecast is used.

Permission to resell, online distribution territories, use of brand assets, exclusivity restrictions and any supplier's willingness to trade must be checked. A public consumer listing is not reseller approval or a wholesale offer. No brand has been contacted for this concept.

Slides 06–07

Follow one real product. Don’t fill the gaps.

The slide 07 reference is the real Honest Basics Basic T-shirt listing above; the drawing is generic. Product description and composition are brand reported. Our current record does not establish independently verified batch chain of custody, a wage outcome, resale rights or availability for this store. This means “not established here”, not a finding that the brand lacks such evidence.

The online collection and evidence filters are design proposals. Keep these statuses distinct:

  • Observed fact: what a dated source actually showed, with its scope.
  • Brand reported: a named company's declaration, without upgrading it to independent verification.
  • Independently checked: identify who checked which claim, date, product/batch coverage and limitations. The mockup does not award this status to either label.
  • Unknown / not established in our record: visible evidence gaps, not a negative judgement about a supplier.
  • Concept: our proposed layout, selection and service, not actual goods on offer.

For a product considered for sale, request evidence for fibre → processing → sewing/finishing → import/use, preserving missing stages and source dates. A supplier invoice is not worker pay. Material or facility certification alone does not establish living wages for every worker. Never publish payroll, worker identities or sensitive grievance records.

Open Supply Hub is an existing facility-identity reference, not proof of product chain of custody or wage certification. No new verification platform is required to sketch the first evidence panel.

Slide 08

Actual purchases. Honest denominators.

Before trading gates pass, use interviews and clearly nontransactional catalogue/selection tests to learn about style, information and price acceptance. They do not establish completed-purchase conversion or revenue. No payments, preorders or customer analytics are collected by this presentation.

After lawful commerce is ready, compare standard product presentation with a collection/evidence treatment. Both must show required product, price and consumer information. Hold product, price, availability, channel and traffic as comparable as possible. Randomise eligible visits if feasible; otherwise label the weaker comparison and its confounders.

  • Conversion: completed purchases / defined eligible visits, not clicks on a mock button. Define payment completion and cancellations consistently.
  • Basket: retained ex-VAT net sales / retained orders; also show items per order. Define discounts, refunds, delivery revenue and VAT treatment.
  • Returns: returned orders / dispatched orders for a mature cohort; separately report partial returns, item counts and refund values.
  • Contribution: retained revenue less properly allocated variable costs; report per order and per eligible visit, with cohort totals. Include failed/refunded orders' costs rather than dropping them.

Agree the learning question, useful commercial effect and measurement definitions before testing. Track service/research time, collect only necessary lawful data, and wait through return windows and processing lag. Small samples may be directional, not statistically decisive. No conversion, uplift, wage effect or demand result is claimed.

Slides 04 / 10

Product sales. Not imaginary margin.

Intended revenue is selling the selected labels' products through a branded store—not assumed affiliate commissions. The principal/agent role and merchant responsibilities are undecided, so gross versus net accounting presentation requires review once the contractual route is known. No revenue, annual recurring revenue, gross-margin rate or profit forecast is supplied.

For an agreed route, reconcile a mature cohort: Retained sales revenue, net of refunds and with correct VAT treatment − agreed product costs − per-order freight / fulfilment / packaging − payment fees and costs of failed or refunded orders − return handling / impairment where applicable (include costs incurred on refunded orders too) − variable service and acquisition costs = contribution before remaining operating costs Then fund systems, research/verification, team time, accounting, finance and other overhead before profit.

Classify fixed and variable research/service costs consistently; do not subtract the same cost twice. Identify cash deposits, supplier payment timing, tax recovery, customer refunds and settlement delays separately from profit. Any stock route also needs markdown/unsold-stock risk. A no-stock route may have higher per-order costs; inventory avoided is not profit earned.

No current supplier/carrier/payment quotation supports a numerical unit margin. The previous presentation's hypothetical consolidated-stock arithmetic has been removed from this revised pitch and narration; it is not a model for supplier fulfilment or no-stock trading. The original private research remains historical and is not changed by this presentation.

Slides 12–14

Design the first online collection.

  1. Define: target customer, narrow branded catalogue/edit, existing-label shortlist, desired quality/fit and evidence floor.
  2. Validate before orders: current access and stockists, reseller/distribution rights, sample quality, evidence, written quote, viable route, merchant responsibilities, Iceland shipping/returns/VAT and lawful commerce.
  3. Learn without pretending to sell: test customer selection, comprehension and price acceptance using clear concepts. Do not call interest or button clicks orders.
  4. Only if ready: separately approve commercial execution and numeric cash/loss limits. Test actual orders and unit economics, reconcile returns and decide expand, revise or stop. No mandatory stock quantity or arbitrary duration.

Retail contributions proposed: digital range, styling, hierarchy, fit judgement and customer understanding. Technology contributions proposed: commerce, evidence presentation and useful cost/order measurements. No appointments, workload, equity, compensation or liability agreed. An own-label range is an optional later roadmap, only after demand, evidence and operations are proven.
