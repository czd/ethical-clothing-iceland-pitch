01 / The possibility

Beautiful clothes.
A shorter distance.

There are beautiful clothes we never get to see. Small labels. Passionate makers. Pieces beyond the familiar shop windows.

What if we brought some of them closer—to people in Iceland and Norway?

02 / The connection

Closer to the people
behind them.

Not an anonymous chain. Real relationships with people closer to production, including smaller workshops beyond the usual routes.

Let’s understand where a garment comes from, who helped make it, and how payment moves along its journey. Then make that story visible.

03 / The collection

Clothes people want.
A story they can follow.

A knit you reach for. Trousers that fit beautifully. A dress that feels like you.

Our own online store, bringing together selected existing labels: good design, lasting quality, and a fair-price ambition. A shorter chain might help create value—not a promise of cheaper clothes.

The clothes are the desire. The story is another reason to love them.

04 / The partnership

Your experience.
My curiosity.
Something we shape together.

You understand the whole business, from a beautiful range to the judgement that earns customers. I bring curiosity, connections to explore, and technology to help us build.

Not an idea handed to you. We’d shape the big questions as equals: what we sell, who we reach, how it’s priced—and whether it’s worth doing at all.

05 / The invitation

Could we build something
we believe in?

Something with taste, purpose, and room to make our own decisions.

Let’s discover a few makers. Choose a first collection. See what excites us both.

No grand commitment. Just the possibility of making something good—together.

The makers, the collection, and the practical model are still ours to explore.
